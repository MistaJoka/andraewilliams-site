## What it looks like

Restraint is the ability to leave a want unsatisfied without drama. The savage can stop at one drink, or none, on any night of the year. The phone sits face down for hours and is not checked mid-conversation. There is no substance, screen, food, or habit in their life that they could not drop for thirty days on a day's notice — and they have proven it, on purpose, more than once.

Temper is the clearest test. The savage has not raised their voice in anger at another person in years. Provocation is received, felt, and answered on a delay chosen by them.

Impulse purchases, impulse messages, impulse opinions: all pass through a gate. The 2 a.m. email waits for 9 a.m. The cart sits for a week. The retort is written and deleted. What reaches the world is what the savage chose, not what the moment produced.

## The gap

The gap looks like "I can quit any time" from someone who has never tried. It looks like the nightly two glasses that are always two, a screen-time report nobody opens, the outburst that is later called passion. It looks like a credit-card statement full of things that were needed for one evening.

## How to get there

- Audit the vices honestly. List every substance, habit, and screen that has a daily claim on you, with the real weekly figure next to each: units, hours, pounds, minutes.
- Run a thirty-day abstinence from each, one at a time, once a year. Anything you cannot drop for thirty days without distress is not a habit; it is an owner. Treat it as one.
- Set hard ceilings and keep them: alcohol within the local low-risk guideline and zero on at least four nights a week; phone out of the bedroom; no notifications that are not from a person.
- Install a delay on every impulse channel. Twenty-four hours on any purchase over a set threshold, ten minutes on any message written in anger, one night on any decision made tired.
- Train the temper the way you train anything: identify the triggers, rehearse the response, review every loss the same day, in writing.

## Measures

- Dated record of at least one thirty-day abstinence completed in the last twelve months, for each vice on your list.
- Weekly screen time and alcohol units known to the exact number, not the impression.
- Zero instances in the last twelve months of shouting at, insulting, or physically intimidating another person. If there was one, it is logged with date and what was done about it.
- Zero purchases in the last ninety days you could not justify to a partner or accountant within ten seconds.
