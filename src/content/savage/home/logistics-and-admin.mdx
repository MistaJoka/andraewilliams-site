## What it looks like

The savage can produce any important document in under two minutes: passport, birth certificate, insurance policies, vehicle title, lease or deed, tax records for the last seven years, warranties, medical records, and the contact details of every professional they rely on. Originals live in one fireproof, known location. Scans live in an encrypted, backed-up folder (HME·05) with a consistent naming scheme.

Every expiry and renewal is in the calendar with a reminder at least sixty days out: passport, driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, domain names, subscriptions, professional certifications, memberships. Nothing lapses. Nothing auto-renews without being reviewed once a year.

The calendar is the single source of truth. If it is not in the calendar, it does not exist. Every commitment made — in person, by message, by email — is entered within the hour. The savage is never double-booked and never late because of a scheduling error.

The inbox is processed, not browsed. Every message gets one of four outcomes: do it (under two minutes), schedule it, delegate it, or delete it. Processing happens at fixed times — twice daily is the ceiling, not all day. Inbox count at the end of the weekly reset (HME·02) is zero or within single digits. Unsubscribe is used aggressively.

## The gap

The gap looks like a passport discovered expired at the airport. It looks like forty minutes searching for an insurance policy number. It looks like an inbox of 4,000 unread used as a to-do list. It looks like commitments kept in memory and a calendar that is half-true. It looks like three subscriptions paying for services nobody has opened in a year. It looks like admin as a permanent low-grade anxiety instead of a closed loop.

## How to get there

- One afternoon: gather every document, scan it, name it `YYYY-MM-DD_type_description`, file the originals. Never let it drift again.
- Enter every expiry date into the calendar now with a sixty-day reminder. Audit subscriptions while you are there.
- Capture rule: any commitment goes in the calendar before the conversation ends.
- Inbox processing at two fixed times per day; notifications off outside them.
- Annual admin review — one block in the same month every year — for insurance, subscriptions, beneficiaries, and contact lists.

## Measures

- Any named document retrievable in under two minutes, timed.
- Every renewable item in the calendar with a sixty-day reminder: list exists, last audited within twelve months.
- Lapsed or expired documents, registrations, or policies in the last twelve months: zero.
- Inbox count at the end of the last weekly reset: single digits.
- Missed or double-booked commitments in the last ninety days: zero.
