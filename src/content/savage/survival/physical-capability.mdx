## What it looks like

Strength (BDY·01) and conditioning (BDY·02) are the base; this standard is what they are for. The savage can swim 400 metres continuously, tread water for ten minutes, and swim 50 metres in clothes.

The savage can carry an adult of their own bodyweight 50 metres in a fireman's or drag carry, climb over a two-metre wall unassisted, pull themselves out of a pool or over a ledge with arms alone, and descend a rope or ladder under control. A loaded pack of 20 kg moves with the savage for 10 km at a brisk walk.

Falling is a trained skill. The savage has practised breakfalls — forward, backward, sideways — on mats until the body does them without thinking, because the ground is the most common thing that will ever hurt you. Balance is kept: a single-leg stand for 60 seconds, eyes closed.

Under adrenaline, the body still works. Hands do fine tasks after a sprint. Breathing recovers on command. The savage has experienced maximal effort often enough that the first time it is needed is not the first time it has happened.

## The gap

The gap is a gym body that cannot swim. It is a deadlift that has never carried a person. It is a fall on an icy pavement that ends in a broken wrist because nobody ever taught the arms what to do. It is turning up to an emergency with a heart rate at 180 and no plan for getting it down.

## How to get there

- Swim lessons if the 400 metres is in doubt. Then swim weekly until it is easy, and test the clothed 50 metres twice a year.
- Add carries to training: farmer's carries, sandbag carries, partner drags. Monthly, move a person.
- Learn breakfalls from a judo or parkour coach. Twenty minutes a week on mats for three months; then quarterly refreshers.
- Practise climbing out: wall, ledge, pool edge. Bouldering once a week builds the pulling and the grip.
- Train stress: interval sessions ending in a fine-motor task — tie a knot, dial a number, write a sentence — while the heart rate is still high.

## Measures

- 400 m continuous swim and 10 minutes treading water: tested and dated within the last 12 months.
- 50 m clothed swim: completed within the last 12 months.
- Bodyweight partner carry over 50 m: completed within the last 90 days.
- Two-metre wall cleared unassisted: yes/no, tested within the last 12 months.
- Breakfalls (three directions) demonstrated to a coach's satisfaction within the last 12 months.
- 10 km with a 20 kg pack, under 2 hours, dated within the last 12 months.
