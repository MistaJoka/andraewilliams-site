## What it looks like

Tax is not a mystery that happens to you every April. The savage knows the shape of their own tax picture: effective rate and marginal rate, both stated correctly, both distinct in the savage's head from each other. The gap between the two is understood, not guessed at, and it drives real decisions — timing, account choice, structure.

Structure is chosen on purpose. Income is earned through whichever legal form actually fits how it is earned — employee, sole proprietor, partnership, or a formal entity — reviewed as income changes, not defaulted into once and forgotten. Tax-advantaged accounts are filled before taxable ones (see MNY·04). Nothing here is a specific tax play; it is the general discipline of not leaving a legal structure on the table out of inertia.

A professional is retained, not summoned in a panic. The relationship runs all year: a mid-year planning conversation before decisions are irreversible, and a filing conversation that reviews the return line by line rather than rubber-stamping it. Records are organised as they happen (see MNY·01), so the professional is doing tax work, not archaeology. The savage asks questions until the answer is understood — never signs what they cannot explain back in one sentence.

## The gap

The gap looks like a shoebox of receipts handed over in March. It looks like a marginal rate confidently misquoted as the effective rate, and a refund celebrated as a win instead of recognised as a free loan to the government. It looks like the same entity structure used for a decade after the income outgrew it, and a tax preparer who has never once heard from the client between January and January.

## How to get there

- Calculate this year's effective rate and marginal rate from the actual return. Write both down.
- Book a mid-year call with a tax professional — not the preparer's busy season, a real planning conversation, before the year's decisions are locked in.
- Review the entity or structure income is earned through once a year. Ask directly: does this still fit, or has income outgrown it.
- Keep the ledger (see MNY·01) current all year so nothing is reconstructed from memory at filing.
- Fill every tax-advantaged account to its limit before anything taxable (see MNY·04).
- Read the return before signing it. If a line cannot be explained back in one sentence, ask until it can.
- File early. An extension is a deliberate tool used on purpose, never a symptom of disorganisation.

## Measures

- Effective and marginal tax rate for the current year known and correctly stated.
- Mid-year planning conversation with a tax professional dated within the last twelve months.
- Entity or income structure reviewed in writing within the last twelve months.
- Zero late filings or disorganisation-driven extensions in the last five years.
- Tax-advantaged accounts filled to their annual limit (see MNY·04, Measures).
