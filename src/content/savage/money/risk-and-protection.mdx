## What it looks like

The savage has asked the question most people avoid: what happens to the money if I die, cannot work, get sued, lose the house, or have my accounts drained? Every answer is written down, and none of them is "I do not know."

Insurance is used as designed: to transfer catastrophic risk and decline annoying risk. Term life at ten to fifteen times income while anyone depends on it. Own-occupation disability covering at least sixty percent of earnings, because disability before retirement is far likelier than early death. Health cover that caps the worst year at a number the reserve absorbs. Property and liability at full replacement with an umbrella above it. Deductibles as high as the reserve allows (see MNY·03); the savage self-insures everything small and pays nobody to carry a broken phone.

The estate is documented: a will, powers of attorney for finance and health, beneficiaries on every account checked annually, and a one-page sheet telling a named person what exists and where. Accounts are locked down — unique passwords, non-SMS second factors, credit frozen at every bureau, and a standing rule of trusting no inbound call about money. The whole structure is reviewed on a fixed date each year and within thirty days of any life event.

## The gap

The gap looks like life insurance through work that ends the day the job does. It looks like no disability cover because "I'm healthy." It looks like a will that was going to get written after the second child, four years ago. It looks like extended warranties on appliances and no umbrella on the house, one password reused everywhere, and a spouse who does not know which bank the savings are in.

## How to get there

- Write the five catastrophe scenarios — death, disability, liability, property loss, account compromise — and the current answer to each. Blank answers are the to-do list.
- Buy term life and own-occupation disability outside any employer within sixty days if either is missing. Size to the formulas above, not to the premium.
- Raise every deductible to the largest amount the reserve absorbs without notice. Cancel every warranty and small-item policy.
- Execute the will and both powers of attorney this quarter. Check beneficiaries every January.
- Freeze credit at every bureau. Move every financial login to a password manager with a unique password and a non-SMS second factor.
- Write the one-page sheet for the named person and walk them through it in person.
- Put the annual review on the calendar, same date every year.

## Measures

- Term life divided by gross income: ten or above while dependents exist.
- Own-occupation disability cover at sixty percent of earnings or above, not tied to an employer.
- Umbrella liability in force at or above net worth.
- Will and both powers of attorney executed; beneficiaries verified within the last twelve months.
- Credit frozen at every bureau; every financial account on a unique password with a non-SMS second factor.
- Named person can state, unprompted, where the money lives.
- Annual review dated within the last twelve months.
