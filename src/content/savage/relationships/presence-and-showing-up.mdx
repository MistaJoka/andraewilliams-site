## What it looks like

Every standard in this domain runs on the same fuel: time, attention, and memory. The savage spends all three on purpose.

Presence looks like a phone that is face down, silent, and in another room when someone is talking to you. It looks like eye contact, questions that follow from what was just said, and never once checking a screen mid-sentence. People leave a conversation with the savage feeling like the only person in the building, because for that hour they were.

Showing up looks like attendance. The funeral, the hospital, the opening night, the kid's match, the retirement drinks — the savage is physically in the room for the moments that matter to the people who matter, and treats the cost of getting there as the price of the relationship.

Remembering looks like details written down and used. Names of partners and children. The date of the surgery. The interview on Thursday. The wine they liked.

## The gap

The gap looks like nodding at a story while reading a notification. It looks like "I was so sorry I couldn't make it" as a recurring line. It looks like asking "how's your mum?" six months after she died. It looks like birthdays acknowledged by the group chat's reminder.

## How to get there

- Phone out of reach during any one-on-one conversation. Not face down on the table — gone.
- Keep a people file: one note per close person with partner, children, dates, current situation, and what they last told you. Update it after every substantive conversation.
- Put every birthday, death anniversary, and known upcoming event — surgery, interview, court date — in the calendar with a reminder the day before.
- Attend by default. A major life event of an inner-circle person or close family member is declined only for a competing one.
- Follow up on the specific thing. Message on the day of the interview, the morning after the surgery, the week after the funeral when everyone else has left.

## Measures

- Screen checks during the last ten one-on-one conversations: zero. Ask the other person if you are unsure.
- Major events of inner circle and close family in the last twelve months: attended, with a list of any missed and the reason.
- People file exists and was updated within the last thirty days. Yes or no.
- Birthdays of inner circle and immediate family missed in the last year: zero.
- Follow-ups sent on the day of a known event in the last ninety days: list them. Fewer than the events known is a miss.
