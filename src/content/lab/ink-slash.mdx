A stroke is a signed distance to a line segment, thresholded. Everything that makes it read as ink rather than a rectangle is the modulation: width tapered by `sin(along*π)` for pressure, a 1D-noise edge for dry brush, and opacity draining from the tail forward so the stroke runs out.
