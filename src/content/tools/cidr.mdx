Every field recomputes on keystroke — there's no submit button because there's nothing to submit to. The whole thing is `computeCidr()` in [cidr.js](https://github.com/MistaJoka/andraewilliams-site/blob/main/src/scripts/tools/cidr.js): parse the octets and prefix, derive the mask, and the rest — network, broadcast, wildcard, usable range — falls out of AND/OR/NOT against that mask.

The one sharp edge is `/0`: a 32-bit left-shift by 32 is a no-op in JavaScript, not zero, because shift amounts are masked to 5 bits. Left as the general formula it would silently return the wrong mask for the whole-internet case, so `/0` gets its own branch instead.
