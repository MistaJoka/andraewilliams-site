Most homelab content starts in the middle: someone shows you their rack
and their Grafana wall, and you leave with a shopping list instead of a
plan.

This series starts from zero — before the hardware, at the question of
what a lab is actually for. Then it builds one machine at a time, in the
order that keeps every step useful on its own: a hypervisor so
experiments are cheap to undo, a network you can reach from anywhere
without opening a port, storage you can afford to lose, and only then
the fun workloads.

Every part records what broke, because that is the part the polished
build videos cut.
